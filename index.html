Privacy Policy for Follow ME Up!

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 • Extension Version: 1.1.0

Follow ME Up! ("the Extension") is a browser extension designed to provide an inbox-focused workspace and assist users in sending user-initiated follow-up messages within Meta Business Suite (https://business.facebook.com/).

1. Information We Collect and Process

  • License & Authentication Information: When you activate or use the Extension, your license key and a randomly generated browser-profile identifier (machineId, formatted as FB-...) are stored locally in local extension storage and transmitted to https://fb-followup-license.alexis-diaz050898.workers.dev to verify license validity, device binding, and expiration status.
  • Inbox & Website Content (Processed Locally): To identify quiet conversations, display an inbox-only view, and populate user-initiated follow-up messages, the Extension reads and interacts with conversation lists and message composers on business.facebook.com. Conversation history and sent-message tracking are stored locally in your browser's localStorage and are not uploaded to our licensing server.
  • Extension Preferences & State: View mode preferences (inbox-only vs. full view), active tab references, and update check timestamps are stored locally via local extension storage.
  • Update Checks & Standard Network Metadata: The Extension periodically requests /version.json from our Worker endpoint to check for newer releases. Like all standard HTTPS requests, Cloudflare's edge network transiently processes the caller's IP address for routing and security.

2. How We Use Information

We use the processed information strictly to:

  • Authenticate and enforce single-device license activation and expiration rules.
  • Deliver the core inbox-only layout and user-initiated follow-up automation on Meta Business Suite.
  • Notify you when an updated version of the Extension is available.

3. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade user data or Meta Business Suite conversation content to third parties. Licensing requests are processed exclusively through our Cloudflare Workers backend.

4. Data Retention and Control

You can deactivate your license key at any time within the Extension's side panel, which clears the stored key from local extension storage. Uninstalling the Extension removes all locally stored extension data, and clearing site data for facebook.com removes any local sent-history records.
